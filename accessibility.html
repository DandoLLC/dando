Accessibility

DANDO wants this site to be usable by everyone, and works toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA.

What the site does

Pages use headings, labeled navigation and a skip link, and can be used with a keyboard. Colors are chosen for readable contrast in both the light and dark themes. The example report image on the homepage has a text version next to it.

If something is hard to use

Email daniel@dandollc.com and say what you were trying to do. I will provide the information another way and fix the page where I can.

Booking page

The booking button opens a Google Calendar page, which Google builds and maintains. If it doesn’t work for you, email instead and I will set up the call directly.
