What you get

  1. A review of your daily bottlenecks, such as intake, renewals or client email, to pick the one task where AI saves the most time
  2. A tool that fits the software you already use
  3. Setup of the first tool, with your team
  4. A one-page AI policy: what data stays out, and who reviews AI work before it goes out
  5. A written before-and-after note on the time it saves
Who it’s for

Local and online businesses of about 5 to 50 people

It fits businesses that run on email, documents and scheduling. Bookkeeping firms, independent insurance agencies, property managers, real estate teams and home-service companies with office staff are good examples.

The first task is one your team repeats every day, such as answering inquiry emails, writing quotes or preparing standard documents. Businesses that hold health records aren’t a fit yet.

How it runs

About three weeks from the first call to the written result

  1. 1

    A first call

    You tell me how the business runs and we pick one everyday task.

  2. 2

    Setup

    I choose the tool, using one you already pay for when possible. A 90-minute session sets it up with your team, including a check before anything goes to a customer.

  3. 3

    Measure

    After two weeks, I write up how the task runs now against how it ran before.

  4. 4

    Wrap-up

    A last call goes over the result and the final policy. A short check-in follows two weeks later.
